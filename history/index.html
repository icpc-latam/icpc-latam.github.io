Latin American Championship (PdA): brief reports

Based on the Maratona SBC de Programação history and ScoreLatam. Full results on each edition page.

2026 – Santiago, Chile

UFMG “ooga booga” won with 8 problems (786 penalty minutes) and became two-time Latin American champion. Gold: UFMG (BR), UP GDL (MX), UNC (AR), UChile (CL). 44 teams, 12 of them Brazilian. World Finals: Dubai.

2025 – Salvador, Bahia, Brazil

Hosted by UFBA, March 13-17, 2025. USP “Faça o WM” won with 9 problems; UNICAMP and UNI-FC (Peru) completed the top 3. 42 teams, 13 of them Brazilian. At the World Finals in Baku UFMG was the best Latin American team (6 problems).

2024 – Guadalajara, Mexico

First edition, hosted by ITESO, March 14-18, 2024. UFMG won with 8 problems and 900 minutes; UBA-FCEN “InChaVoLa” was second, one minute behind. At the World Finals in Astana UBA was the best Latin American team (17th place).
