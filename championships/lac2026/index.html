The Latin American Championship was held in Santiago, Chile. Maratona SBC reports 44 participating teams, 12 of them Brazilian; the final scoreboard lists 41 ranked teams. UFMG, which also won the first edition in Guadalajara (2024), became two-time Latin American champion. Gold medals went to UFMG (Brazil), UP GDL (Mexico), UNC (Argentina) and UChile (Chile).
