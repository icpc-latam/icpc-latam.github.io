Official Rules

PDF2026-2027 Rules: LAR 2026 Regionals & Promotion to the LAC 2027 Championship2026-2027 ICPC Latin America - Promotion Rules.pdf · 140 KB

Teams qualify through the 2026 Regionals (LAR), held on November 7th, 2026. Under the standard criteria 42 teams qualify (40 base slots plus 2 all-female slots); the hosts provide 38 additional slots. In total, 80 teams will qualify for the 2027 Championship.

Teams can test the image that will be used in the contest. The practice image boots the same environment as the contest (Maratona Linux, from a USB drive), with one difference: it has free access to the internet, while on contest day the firewall allows only the contest systems. Testing lets teams learn the editors and tools and find hardware problems while there is still time to fix them. See How to Test the Competition System.
