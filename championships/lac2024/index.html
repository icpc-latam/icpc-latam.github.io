The first Latin American Championship was held on the ITESO campus in Guadalajara, Mexico. UFMG won with 8 problems solved and 900 penalty minutes; UBA-FCEN (“InChaVoLa”), also with 8 problems, was second with a penalty just one minute higher. Brazil sent 16 teams.

At the 2023/24 ICPC World Finals in Astana, Kazakhstan (September 15-20, 2024), 18 Latin American teams took part. UBA was the best Latin American team (17th place, 7 problems solved).
